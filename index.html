Questions

Frequently asked

What documents do I need?

A valid CNIC or passport, and a driving license for self drive. We keep copies on file for the trip only.

Is there a mileage limit?

Daily rentals include a fair daily distance. Extra kilometres are charged at a fixed rate, shown in your quote.

Can I change my dates after booking?

Yes, if the car is free. Message us on WhatsApp at least 12 hours before pick-up.

What if the car breaks down?

Call or WhatsApp us right away. We send a replacement car or a recovery vehicle as soon as possible.
